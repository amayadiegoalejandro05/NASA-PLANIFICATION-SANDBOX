This product was assembled from data gathered using the Viking Orbiter Imaging Systems aboard the Viking 1 and Viking 2 orbiters. Identical vidicon cameras aboard both orbiters were equipped with 475-mm focal length telephoto lenses, yielding fields of view of 1.54° X 1.69°. From an orbital altitude of 1500 km, each frame covers a minimum area on the surface of 40 X 44 km. Six selectable filters allowed color images to be acquired.
